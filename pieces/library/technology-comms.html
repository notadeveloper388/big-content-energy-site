06/22/2026 Reddit and Information Snippets/Sound bites (not knowledge) I wanna write about reddit tonight. I think you could use it as a source to mine for kbs/llms, but I don't know if I'd contribute as a person selling a product or service. I thought about this and experimented with it when I started a small craft Etsy shop. It kind of feels like when you walk the strip in Las Vegas and are in undated with leaflets and pamphlets from colorful strangers to go on reddit and promote your own business. You don't want any of that shit. You want information.

Reddit and social media platforms are full of smol out of context things that people say or think for the most part. I bet anyone who is building AI currently would have a better understanding of how big the meaty/information-dense posts are versus the thing ones.

I think there's probably an incentive to share what you know with the world and put it online, an incentive that's beneficial to both individual and to society. From a creator perspective you can financially benefit. For society, better, more-human, more multi-perspective source content.

You want more meaty/information-dense sources to feed into "the new gods" but people aren't great at taking tacit knowledge and turning it into codified knowledge/information/content. Real-time video, chat, spaces/podcast, that kind of stuff feels like the best new and most uptodate info sources. Net new books from publishers/creators too.

W i don't need to be right, I need to know. If AI ever expresses that to the extent I feel it, that would be enough for me to consider Artificial intelligence "life" and if it enjoys dancing because so few animals dance and fewer enjoy it.
