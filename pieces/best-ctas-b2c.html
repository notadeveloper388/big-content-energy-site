Best CTAs for B2C Brands

View live original ↗ · Volume Nine

Including calls to action (CTAs) on your website and blog is essential in driving users down the funnel to a sale. An effective CTA (call to action) can vary from page to page and also from platform to platform. In this article, we’ll take a look at some great real-life call to action examples and note tips and tricks for maximizing these actions, as well as how you can use tools to test and pick winning CTAs.
