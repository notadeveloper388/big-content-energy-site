Questions

Is ANON POST MAIL anonymous?

Yes. The postcard carries no sender name and no return address. The recipient sees only your message, the card front, and their own address.

How much does it cost to send an anonymous postcard?

$2.99 per 4×6 postcard, including printing and USPS First-Class postage. There are no subscriptions or extra fees.

How long does an anonymous postcard take to arrive?

Most postcards arrive in 5–7 business days by USPS First-Class Mail. Delivery is to US addresses only.

What can't I send?

Anything not lawful under US law, such as true threats, harassment, or extortion, is refused and refunded. Everything protected as free speech goes through. Postcards are unsealed, so anyone handling the card may read it.
