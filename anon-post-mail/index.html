Questions

Is ANON POST MAIL anonymous?

Yes. The postcard carries no sender name and no return address. The recipient sees only your message, the card front, and their own address.

How much does it cost to send an anonymous postcard?

$2.99 per 4×6 postcard, including printing and USPS First-Class postage. There are no subscriptions or extra fees.

How long does an anonymous postcard take to arrive?

Most postcards arrive in 5–7 business days by USPS First-Class Mail. Delivery is to US addresses only.

What can't I send?

Anything not lawful under US law, such as true threats, harassment, or extortion, is refused and refunded. Everything protected as free speech goes through. Postcards are unsealed, so anyone handling the card may read it.

What paper is the postcard printed on?

A 4×6 postcard on heavy 100–120 lb cover card stock with a gloss finish, printed in black and white by a commercial mail house.

Will the recipient know who sent it?

No. The card shows no sender name and no return address.

What happens if the postcard can't be delivered?

It does not come back. There is no return address, so undeliverable cards are not returned.

What does the postcard look like?

A 4×6 heavy gloss card printed in black and white, with your choice of 6 front designs.

How long can my message be?

As long as it fits on the card, usually about 240 characters. The page tells you if your message is too long, and nothing is ever cut off.

How is the postcard mailed?

By USPS First-Class Mail.

Where can I send a postcard?

US addresses only.

What happens to messages that aren't lawful?

They are refused and refunded.

Who can read my postcard?

Postcards are unsealed, so anyone who handles the card may read the message.

Can I track my postcard?

No. Tracking is not available.
